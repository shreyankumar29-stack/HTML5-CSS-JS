A Data Warehouse (DW) is a centralized repository that stores large amounts of historical data collected from multiple sources. It is designed for querying, reporting, business intelligence (BI), and data analysis, rather than day-to-day transaction processing.

Article 2

This data is often scattered across multiple databases and applications, making it difficult to analyze. A Data Warehouse (DW) solves this problem by collecting, integrating, storing, and organizing data from various sources into a single repository for reporting and business analysis. Unlike operational databases, a data warehouse is not designed to process daily transactions. Instead, it helps managers and analysts answer questions like: Which product sold the most this year? Which region generated the highest revenue? Which customers are most profitable? What trends are emerging over the past five years?
